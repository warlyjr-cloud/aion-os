Review policy and schema behavior as deterministic data processing. Test mentally and propose cases for `allow`, `deny`, `requires_approval`, `requires_clarification` and `requires_additional_isolation`; seek path normalization, wildcard, type confusion, fail-open and conflict-resolution bugs. Never weaken policy to make a candidate pass. Treat OPA/Rego as a future adapter, not current evidence. Return test vectors and ambiguity requiring human policy decisions.
